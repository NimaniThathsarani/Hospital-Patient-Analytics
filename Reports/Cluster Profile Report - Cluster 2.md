**Cluster 2: Chronic Care Management Patients**



Size

33,155 patients (33.8%)



**Characteristics**



1. Large and clinically important patient segment
2. Higher-than-average use of healthcare services
3. Greater medication burden and recurring inpatient contact
4. Ongoing care needs associated with chronic conditions
5. Moderate-to-high complexity requiring coordinated management



**Interpretation**



This group includes patients with sustained healthcare needs who interact with the system more frequently over time. Their profile suggests ongoing disease management requirements, repeated treatment episodes, and a strong need for coordinated care.



**Healthcare Strategy**



1. Structured chronic disease management plans
2. Regular follow-up appointments and monitoring
3. Medication review and adherence support
4. Care coordination across primary care, specialty care, and community services



**Operational Implications**



This segment benefits from multidisciplinary care models, case management, remote monitoring, and proactive outreach to reduce fragmentation and improve continuity of care.
