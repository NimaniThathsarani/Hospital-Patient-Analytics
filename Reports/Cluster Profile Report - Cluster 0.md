**Cluster 0: Low-Acuity Community Patients**

Size

1,984 patients (2.0%)



**Characteristics**



1. Younger to middle-aged patient population
2. Lower overall clinical complexity
3. Moderate medication use and limited inpatient contact
4. Lower risk of readmission compared with the highest-acuity groups
5. Generally stable health status with manageable ongoing care needs



**Interpretation**



This segment represents patients who are mostly suitable for routine outpatient and community-based care. Their needs are typically focused on preventive services, medication adherence, and regular follow-up rather than intensive hospital-based management.



**Healthcare Strategy**



1. Routine primary care follow-up
2. Medication adherence education and support
3. Preventive screenings and wellness coaching
4. Early outreach for symptom monitoring and continuity of care



**Operational Implications**



This group is best managed through ambulatory care pathways, lower-intensity case management, and digital support tools that help maintain care continuity while reducing avoidable hospital use.
