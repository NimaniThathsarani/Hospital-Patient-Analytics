**Cluster 3: High-Risk Complex Patients**



Size

43,648 patients (44.5%)



**Characteristics**



1. Largest patient segment
2. Highest average age and greatest clinical complexity
3. High number of diagnoses and medications
4. Greater likelihood of prolonged hospitalization and readmission
5. Strong indicators of intensive resource use and higher care needs



**Interpretation**



This cluster represents the most medically vulnerable patients in the dataset. These individuals typically require frequent monitoring, specialized treatment, and highly coordinated care across multiple providers and settings.



**Healthcare Strategy**



1. Comprehensive care management
2. Frequent clinical monitoring and specialist involvement
3. Early intervention for deterioration and readmission risk
4. Personalized discharge planning and transitional care support



**Operational Implications**



This group should be prioritized for high-risk population management programs, intensive care coordination, and targeted resource allocation to reduce preventable complications, recurrent admissions, and care gaps.
