**Cluster 1: Short-Stay Acute Recovery Patients**



Size

19,266 patients (19.6%)



**Characteristics**



1. Relatively younger patients on average
2. Shorter hospital stays
3. Lower medication burden and limited inpatient utilization
4. Moderate diagnostic complexity with a more acute care profile
5. Less need for long-term chronic disease management



**Interpretation**



This cluster reflects patients whose care needs are concentrated around a single acute episode and a relatively quick recovery period. Their main challenge is often successful discharge and avoiding complications during the post-discharge period.



**Healthcare Strategy**



1. Efficient discharge planning
2. Clear medication instructions and reconciliation
3. Prompt post-discharge follow-up
4. Patient education on warning signs and escalation pathways



**Operational Implications**



This segment can be supported effectively through streamlined inpatient workflows, rapid discharge coordination, and targeted outpatient outreach shortly after hospitalization.
